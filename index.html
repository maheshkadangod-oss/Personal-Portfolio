About

I'm Mahesh, a solo founder from Kerala. I run Incubateer, a grant platform that tells founders the truth: which grants they can win, which are worth a look, and which are long shots.

The templates here started as sites for friends: a jeweller, a burger joint, a saree brand and a marketer. Each one is live, so you can click through it before you ask for your own. I build with Claude Code and Google AI Studio, and I keep at the details until a page feels right.
