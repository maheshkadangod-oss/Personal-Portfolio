About

Hi, I'm Mahesh.

I'm a solo founder from Kerala. I run Incubateer, a grant platform that tells founders the truth: which grants they can win, which are worth a look, and which are long shots.

Every site here started as a conversation with a friend about what their business needs. I build with Claude Code and Google AI Studio, and I keep at the details until a page feels right.
