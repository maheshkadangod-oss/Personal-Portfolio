Questions

Straight answers.

Who is Mahesh Kadamkode?

I'm Mahesh Kadamkode, a solo, bootstrapped founder from Kerala, India. I build Incubateer, a grant and funding discovery platform for startup founders, and I make websites for people I believe in.

What is Incubateer?

Incubateer is a grant and funding discovery platform. It indexes 8,500+ grants and programmes, reads each one's eligibility rules and scores them against your startup as a strong match, worth a look or a long shot. The promise is in the tagline: find the grants you'll actually win.

What does honest eligibility mean?

It means a founder hears the odds before spending the time. A long shot gets called a long shot. If a hard rule rules you out, Incubateer says so up front, not three weeks into an application.

Are the websites on this page client work?

They're templates. Each one started as a site for a friend: a jeweller, a burger place, a saree brand, a marketer and an engineer. All of them are live, so you can click through before you ask for one shaped for your business.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. Pick the template closest to what you need, then message me on WhatsApp, call +91 90614 63367 or email maheshkadangod@gmail.com. Tell me what the business does and who it's for.

Do you speak about startup funding?

Yes. I talk about grants, eligibility and why most founders apply for the wrong ones. Write to me with the event, the audience and the date.

Where are you based?

Kerala, India. I work with founders in India, the Middle East and further away, mostly over WhatsApp and email.
